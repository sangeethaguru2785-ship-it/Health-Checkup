Our Journey

Two decades of steady progress

From a single lab room to a full preventive-health center trusted by thousands of families.

Founded in 2005, Stackly began with a simple mission — to make preventive healthcare calm, accurate and genuinely accessible. What started as a two-analyser pathology lab has grown into a complete preventive-health center, yet our promise has never changed: every report belongs to a person, not a number.

Over two decades we invested in people, technology and accreditation — expanding into imaging, cardiac care and 18 specialties, and bringing certified home collection to doorsteps across the metro. Today more than 120 specialists and a caring coordinator team stand behind every checkup, committed to catching risks early and keeping families healthy for the long run.
